Tax & legal research

It cites the article, or it stops.

A research tool a professional can check in seconds is a different thing from a chatbot they cannot. The citation is not a feature, it is the product.

It holds the law, not a summary

23 documents and 4,191 searchable passages: ЗДДС, ЗКПО, ЗДДФЛ, ДОПК, КСО, ЗОП, ЗВО, СБО/ЕБК, real НАП opinions and the double-taxation treaties.

It refuses instead of inventing

Asked a procedure that is not in its documents, it names which steps it cannot ground and stops. For a tax tool this is the most valuable behaviour it has.

It checks its own numbers

If an answer states a decision, an order or an article number that is not in the sources it was given, the answer carries a visible warning listing exactly which references are unverified.

Each firm's documents stay that firm's

Shared law is loaded once and serves everybody. A firm's own internal documents are seen only by that firm.

Built for people who are not AI people

A topic picker with ready-made questions, clarifying questions early, and a turn cap that starts a fresh session rather than letting answers quietly get worse.
