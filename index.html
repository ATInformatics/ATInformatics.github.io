04 / Method

Measured, not asserted.

Every product here reports what it checked. These are the rules it was built under.

Software can report success and still have left the wrong result.

Our ruleA write is not believed until it is read back.

Every change is re-read from the thing it changed.

A fader that moved and a sound that changed are two different facts.

Our ruleMeasurements come from the output, never the setting.

Only one of those two facts is evidence, so only one is used.

A tool that quietly does the wrong thing to your file is worse than one that does nothing.

Our ruleRefusing is a feature.

Wrong project, stale state, a meaningless measurement: each one stops the work and says which.

A number improving is not a reason to change your record.

Our ruleIt proposes, you decide.

The cost of every suggestion is stated next to it.

Marketing pages round up, and nobody can check them.

Our ruleIf it is not proved, it says so.

Including on this website. Where something is unverified, the page says so instead of rounding up.
