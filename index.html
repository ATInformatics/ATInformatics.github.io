05 / Built differently

Measured, not asserted.

The rules everything here is built under, for your business and for our own software.

An assistant that guesses a price or an opening time costs you a customer.

Our ruleRefusing is a feature.

It answers from your approved business facts. When it does not know, it says so and passes the enquiry to you.

A system can say “saved” and still have lost the enquiry.

Our ruleA write is not believed until it is read back.

Our software checks the result, not only the action: an enquiry counts when it is in the record, not when a form said thanks.

A dashboard full of settings is not a business that works.

Our ruleMeasurements come from the output, never the setting.

We count enquiries, bookings and money received where they are connected. A source that is not connected says so.

Automation should not take decisions away from you.

Our ruleIt proposes, you decide.

You choose what the assistant answers and when it hands over to you. Every suggestion has its cost next to it.

Marketing pages round up, and nobody can check them.

Our ruleIf it is not proved, it says so.

Including on this website. Where something is unverified, the page says so instead of rounding up.
