Science, software, and audio engineering

I read immunology and biochemistry, wrote a computational neuroscience dissertation, and worked in a lab. Then I started building the software. I do all of it myself: the automation I sell to businesses, the products, the measurement tools, and the records they were written for. 01

The record.

Education

I read immunology and biochemistry. My dissertation was computational neuroscience: sleep architecture in aged against young mice, and what that comparison says about human sleep. It was a large dataset, and I worked it in MATLAB and XLStat.

02

What it taught me, and what I do with it.

I work to a protocol, and I write them

A lab taught me that a procedure which is almost right is simply wrong, and that an unrepeatable result is not a result. Everything I build now reads back what it wrote instead of reporting success. One of my products reconciled 364 of 364 figures against what a client had actually filed, not against my own output.

I plan the work before I write it

Agent and AI work punishes you hardest for this. I turn a job into steps with the check that closes each one, and I write that down first. It is the same habit as a method section, and it is why I can hand a client a fixed price instead of an estimate.

I run a project end to end, alone

Scope, build, client conversation, delivery, and the support afterwards. There is nobody to escalate to and no integration gap, because there is no hand-off. I decide what is worth building and I carry the consequence when I am wrong.

I analyse data at research scale

A dissertation's worth of it, in MATLAB and XLStat, with a result that had to survive review. I know the difference between a figure that is significant and a figure that is interesting.

I learn a regulated domain properly

I modelled Bulgarian tax and payroll law closely enough to emit declarations a client files with the revenue office. Reading the statute is the job; the code is the easy part.

I instrument a field rather than just working in it

I implemented ITU-R BS.1770 loudness measurement and loudness-matched blind comparison from the standard. Knowing a subject well enough to build its instruments is different from being able to use them.

03

What I have shipped.

AfterTime

The platform: the workstation, the agents, the Studio and the memory. Its live surface drives REAPER: it organises takes, builds the comp, measures the mix and renders, inside the project you already have open.

Closed beta

Payroll

You give it the monthly ведомост your own system already produces. It returns both declarations you file, чл. 73 ЗДДФЛ and чл. 142 ДОПК, in the exact layout you file them, each with its own deadline.

Ready for a client

Cognition

A research assistant for Bulgarian accounting and public-sector finance. It holds the law rather than a summary of it, cites чл./ал./т. next to the answer, and refuses when its sources do not cover the question.

In pilot
