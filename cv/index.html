Software, audio engineering, and the science underneath both

Trained in immunology and biochemistry, with a computational neuroscience dissertation behind me. One person builds all of the rest of it: the automation sold to businesses, the products, the measurement tools, and the records they were written for. 01

What I offer, and what backs it.

Analysis at research scale

A computational neuroscience dissertation: sleep architecture in aged against young mice, and what it says about human sleep. Large data, MATLAB and XLStat, and a result that had to survive review. Freelance analysis work since.

Modelling a regulated domain to the point of filing

Bulgarian tax and payroll law, modelled closely enough to emit declarations a client files. Reconciled 364 of 364 figures against what she had actually submitted, not against my own output.

Whole-stack delivery, with nobody to hand off to

Python backends, a REAPER integration layer, a React frontend, a native macOS app, and the packaging and deployment under all of it. The integration gap is where most projects die; there isn't one.

Evidence as the default, not the audit

Every product states what it checked. Cognition refuses rather than guesses when its sources fall short. A 191-file suite, mutation-proved rather than assumed. Several of its tests exist because a green suite once hid a real defect.

Laboratory and diagnostic practice

A degree in immunology and biochemistry, bench work, and diagnostic testing at volume through the COVID pandemic, where a procedure that is almost right is simply wrong.

Audio engineering deep enough to instrument it

ITU-R BS.1770 loudness measurement and loudness-matched blind comparison, written from the standard rather than bought in. Knowing a field well enough to build its instruments is a different thing from using them.

03

What I have shipped.

AfterTime

The platform: the workstation, the agents, the Studio and the memory. Its live surface drives REAPER: it organises takes, builds the comp, measures the mix and renders, inside the project you already have open.

Closed beta

Payroll

You give it the monthly ведомост your own system already produces. It returns both declarations you file, чл. 73 ЗДДФЛ and чл. 142 ДОПК, in the exact layout you file them, each with its own deadline.

Ready for a client

Cognition

A research assistant for Bulgarian accounting and public-sector finance. It holds the law rather than a summary of it, cites чл./ал./т. next to the answer, and refuses when its sources do not cover the question.
