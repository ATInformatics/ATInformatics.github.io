One person builds all of it: the automation sold to businesses, the products, the measurement tools, and the records they were written for. This page exists for employers and for a Bioinformatics with AI application. It is not linked from anywhere.

01

What I am good at.

Builds the whole stack

Python backends, a REAPER integration layer, a React frontend, a native macOS app, and the packaging and deployment under all of it.

Measures instead of asserting

Every product here reports what it checked. Payroll reconciles against filed declarations; Cognition refuses when its sources fall short. That habit is the point, not a feature.

Domain depth in two fields

Audio engineering deep enough to write the measurement tools, and Bulgarian tax and payroll law modelled closely enough to produce filing-ready declarations.

Ships, then proves it

A 191-file test suite, mutation-proved rather than assumed. Several entries in it exist because a green suite once hid a real defect.

02

What I have shipped.

AfterTime

The platform: the workstation, the agents, the Studio and the memory. Its live surface drives REAPER: it organises takes, builds the comp, measures the mix and renders, inside the project you already have open.

Closed beta

Payroll

You give it the monthly ведомост your own system already produces. It returns both declarations you file, чл. 73 ЗДДФЛ and чл. 142 ДОПК, in the exact layout you file them, each with its own deadline.

Ready for a client

Cognition

A research assistant for Bulgarian accounting and public-sector finance. It holds the law rather than a summary of it, cites чл./ал./т. next to the answer, and refuses when its sources do not cover the question.

In pilot
