Education and experience.

Degree

Dissertation

A computational neuroscience project: the sleep architecture of young against old mice, and how it maps onto human sleep. It involved processing and classifying large volumes of EEG and EMG data (brain and muscle activity) into wake, REM and NREM stages. The methodology meant selecting and applying the right statistical analyses and computing probability scales, for accuracy and significance in the results.

Data analysis, freelance

02

Professional experience and practical skills.

Critical analysis of scientific literature

Four years of academic training taught me not to memorise facts but to analyse complex scientific publications in depth. I can quickly assess the choice of methodology, understand the reasoning behind an approach, and judge critically whether the conclusions are supported by the actual data.

Research interest

The application of machine learning to the immunology literature and to single-cell data. I am inspired by large-scale work such as Domínguez Conde and the Teichmann lab at the Wellcome Sanger Institute, whose 2022 Science paper classified 101 immune populations across 16 tissues with their own automated classifier, CellTypist, feeding the Human Cell Atlas. My current focus is building knowledge graphs over the published literature to surface previously unknown biological connections and generate hypotheses for new experiments: pattern recognition over large text corpora, which I already do in practice in another field.

Data analysis and project management

I analyse large datasets and manage complex projects under tight deadlines. I have a proven ability to extract valuable business insight from large-scale data, delivering verifiable and accurate results for the client. I am strong on organisation: I coordinate many parallel tasks, manage stakeholder expectations, and meet agreed deadlines without compromise.

Protocols and quality control

I have extensive experience working to strictly defined protocols. My approach rests on precision and reproducibility: in this field, "almost right" means "wrong". I focus on automated quality control. As proof, one of our products reached 100% accuracy, reconciling every real value clients had actually filed.

I plan before I execute

I design and structure processes in detail before implementing them. Working with AI and autonomous agents demands absolute algorithmic precision, but the instinct is older than that: it is the scientific method. I break every task into sequential steps with defined validation criteria at each stage, which gives predictable, high-quality results.

Fast learner in a regulated field

I quickly acquire skills in a strictly regulated professional field. The tax-and-payroll work is built in full compliance with the requirements, to generate declarations ready to file with the revenue office. The core of the job is interpreting and applying the legal requirements; the code is the easy part.

I instrument a field, not just work in it

ITU-R BS.1770 loudness measurement and loudness-matched blind comparison, implemented from the published standard. Knowing a field deeply enough to build its instruments is entirely different from simply being able to use them.
