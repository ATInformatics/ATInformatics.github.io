About

One person builds all of it.

One person builds everything here. That is the trade: you deal with the person who wrote it, and you never get handed to an account manager who has to go and ask.

Who you are dealing with

A.T. Informatics is one engineer in Bulgaria. The person who answers your email is the person who writes the code and the person who fixes it at the weekend.

Why the software checks itself

Because the first version did not, and reported success on work it had not done. Everything built since reads back what it wrote. The rules on the home page are not marketing, they are scar tissue.

What you own at the end

Plain files. No licence to renew, no platform to be locked into, no subscription that quietly doubles. If you stop paying for hosting, you keep the site.

What is not offered

Anything needing a team. No 24-hour phone support, no enterprise contract, no SLA with penalties. A one-person shop that promises those is lying about one of them.

Start a conversation.

A project, beta access, or a question about what any of it actually does. It reaches a person.
