About

One point of contact for all of it.

One engineer builds the websites, assistants, dashboards and software. Brand identity comes from our design partner. Either way you deal with one person, and you never get handed to an account manager who has to go and ask.

Who you are dealing with

A.T. Informatics is one engineer, working remotely, with a design partner for brand identity (over ten years in design and marketing). The person who answers your email is the person who writes the code and the person who fixes it at the weekend.

Where the work is done, and under which law

Remotely, for clients in the UK and across the EU. Prices are set for each market and shown in euro or pounds, and the price is agreed on the call before work starts. The terms and the licence are governed by the law of Scotland, and they say so on their own pages rather than in the small print.

Why the software checks itself

Because the first version did not, and reported success on work it had not done. Everything built since reads back what it wrote. The rules on the home page are not marketing, they are scar tissue.

What you own at the end

Plain files. No licence to renew, no platform to be locked into, no subscription that quietly doubles. If you stop paying for hosting, you keep the site.

What is not offered

Anything needing a team. No 24-hour phone support, no enterprise contract, no SLA with penalties. A one-person shop that promises those is lying about one of them.

Start a conversation.

A project, beta access, or a question about what any of it actually does. It reaches a person.
