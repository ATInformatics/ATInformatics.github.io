About us

The team behind the system.

Engineering, brand design and our own AI systems, working as one team. You get one point of contact and one invoice, and the team that builds your work is the team that answers you.

Who builds your work

In-house engineering builds the websites, assistants, dashboards and software. Brand identity comes from our design partner, with over ten years in design and marketing. Our own AI systems do the checking and the routine work, and a person signs off every step.

How we work with you

A short call first. Then a written scope and a fixed price, before any work starts. You see the work while it is built, and you approve it before it goes live.

Where we work, and under which law

Remotely, for clients in the UK and across the EU. Prices are set for each market and agreed on the call. The terms and the licence are governed by the law of Scotland, and they say so on their own pages.

Work that checks itself

Everything we build reads back what it did before it reports that it is done. You get evidence, not adjectives.

What you own at the end

Plain files. No licence to renew, no platform to be locked into, no subscription that quietly doubles. If you stop paying for hosting, you keep the site.

Start a conversation.

A project, beta access, or a question about what any of it actually does. It reaches our team.
